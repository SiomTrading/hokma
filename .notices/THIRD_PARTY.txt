Broker connectors (SPEC-018, block B5). Hokma's own code, written on top of these libraries and public interfaces; nothing is copied from them.

- alpaca-py: Apache-2.0 License. Used by the Alpaca connector for orders, positions and the account.
- ccxt: MIT License. Used by the crypto connector for spot balances and orders at an exchange.
- IBKR web API documentation (Client Portal Web API): the interface that the IBKR connector speaks to the gateway on the student's own computer.

---

Back office charts (equity curve, drawdown and composition): the idea of these three views follows the plots of the open projects
Perspective, D-Tale and btplotting. The charts of Hokma are written from scratch as inline SVG; no code, data or asset of those projects
is included or loaded.

---

Derivative pricing (SVC-options, SPEC-019 block B1; recipe in docs/quant/options-pricing.md). The recipe and the code are Hokma's own; nothing is copied.
The models follow their published papers, cited here as the origin of the formulas: Black and Scholes (1973) and Merton (1973) for the option price with a
dividend yield; Garman and Kohlhagen (1983) for currency options; Cox, Ross and Rubinstein (1979), Jarrow and Rudd (1983), Tian (1993) and Leisen and Reimer
(1996) for the binomial trees; Bjerksund and Stensland (2002) for the American approximation; Reiner and Rubinstein (1991) for barrier options and
Kemna and Vorst (1990) for the geometric average price option; Breeden and Litzenberger (1978) for the risk-neutral density; Gatheral (2004) for the SVI smile.
The pricing libraries FinancePy, tf-quant-finance and the QuantLib bindings were read only for the idea of which model fits which style of option; no code, data or asset of them is included or loaded.

---

Replication statistics (SPEC-019 block B9, rule "realistic expectations" of the desk judge). The rule text is Hokma's own; nothing is copied.
The figures it quotes (a median Sharpe ratio of 0.37 and 48 percent of results with a t statistic above 1.96, out of 4,843 academic papers coded and run) are
self-declared by the maintainer of a public index of replicated academic trading results. They have not been verified here and carry medium confidence.
Hokma uses them only as a prior for the judge, never as a threshold.
