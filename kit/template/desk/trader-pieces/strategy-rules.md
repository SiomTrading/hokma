# Strategy rules

The mandate of the desk is the contract the owner wrote: the cadence, the risk budget, the analysts and the strategies with their horizon. It is listed below the pieces.

Respect the horizon of the strategy when you choose the expiration: the structure must outlive the catalyst by enough days for the thesis to play out and must not carry more time than the horizon. While the premium selling switch of the desk is closed, express a bullish or bearish view with a purchase or a debit spread, and stay out of a neutral view that needs premium selling.

The mandate names no instrument. The symbol of the run is the only one you may propose.
