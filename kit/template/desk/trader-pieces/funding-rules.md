# Rules of a funded account

A funded account has rules of its own and they bind before the mandate does: a daily loss allowance, a trailing drawdown, a cap on the contracts held and a consistency rule on the best day.

The current values for this desk are listed below the pieces when they exist. Read them as given. Do not estimate how much room is left: the sizing module counts it from the book. A rule that is missing is a rule that does not exist for this desk, and a rule whose fact is unknown stops the order.
