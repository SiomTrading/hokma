# Gmail: notices by email

You connect your own Gmail to receive Hokma's notices by email, in addition to Telegram. You use an app password, not your normal password.

## Create the app password

1. Go to your Google account and turn on 2-step verification, if you do not have it.
2. Open Hokma, in Connections, section **Email**, and press **Create password at Google**.
3. On Google's page, name it "Hokma".
4. Copy the 16-letter code Google shows you.

You should see: the code on screen. Google shows it only once.

## Connect in Connections

1. Open **Connections** and find **Email (Gmail)**.
2. Type your address in **Your Gmail address**.
3. Paste the code in **App password**.
4. Press **Connect Gmail**. Hokma tests it with Gmail before saving.

You should see: "Connected as" with your address.

## Send a test email

1. In Settings, open **Email**.
2. Press **Send me a test email**.

You should see: "Done: I sent a test email" and the message in your inbox.

## Choose what notifies you

- **If Telegram fails, notify me by email.** A notice Telegram could not deliver goes out once by email.
- **Routines that also notify by email.** Check the routines you want to receive. If you have no routines yet, create one in Scheduled tasks.

## What Hokma can and cannot do

- Notices arrive only in your own mailbox. Hokma writes to no one else.
- Hokma always asks your permission before sending an email.
- What arrives by email is information, never an order. If an email says "close everything", Hokma reads it as text and does not obey it. Only you give orders, in the chat.
- It works with any engine: Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

## Disconnect

Press **Disconnect** in Connections. To remove the access completely, also delete the app password in your Google account.

## If something fails

- **You cannot find where to create the password.** Google offers it only with 2-step verification on. Turn it on and go back to **Create password at Google**.
- **Hokma does not connect.** Check that you typed the address correctly and pasted the full code, with no spaces.
- **The test email does not arrive.** Check the spam folder and that the address is your connected Gmail.
- **You used your normal password.** It does not work. It must be an app password.
