# First steps

Hokma is a team of AI agents that works for your fund. You talk to the orchestrator, and it routes the work to the
right agent. Nothing here trades real money by itself.

## Your first day

1. **Talk to your orchestrator.** Open Chat, write what you want to work on and send it.
2. **Create your first project.** In the right panel press the plus button, give it a name and a type.
3. **Upload a photo of a chart.** Drag an image into the chat, paste it, or press the plus button in the message box.

The card "Your first day with Hokma" in Learn marks each step as you complete it.

## Where things are

- **Left menu:** new conversation, search, your pinned and recent conversations, and the sections.
- **Top tabs:** every screen opens as a tab. The plus button opens modules and assets.
- **Right panel:** your working folder and your projects.
- **Terminal:** the bar at the bottom. Open it with Ctrl and the backtick key.
- **Settings:** the gear at the bottom of the left menu.

## Good to know

- Your work lives in the working folder shown in the right panel. Hokma never deletes it.
- The agents ask you before they do anything sensitive. You answer inside the chat.
- Press Ctrl K to search your conversations and Ctrl N to start a new one.
