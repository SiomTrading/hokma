# Chat

The chat is where you work with your agents. Answers appear as they are written, and you can stop them at any time.

## Sending messages

- Write in the message box and press Enter to send. Shift and Enter makes a new line.
- While an answer is being written the Send button becomes Stop. Stopping keeps what was already written.
- A permission card appears when an agent needs your approval to do something. Approve or reject it there.

## Attachments

- Press the plus button, drag files into the chat, or paste an image from the clipboard.
- Images and files are saved with the conversation.
- Use **Save in project** on an attachment or on an answer to copy it into the folder of the active project.

## Model

The model selector in the message box offers Haiku (fastest, uses less), Sonnet (the default) and Opus (the most
capable, spends your weekly allowance faster). The choice is saved per conversation.

## Conversations

- Search by title or by content from the box at the top of the left menu.
- Pin a conversation to keep it at the top, rename it, or archive it from its menu.
- The conversation shows which project and which agent it is talking to.

## Commands

Type a slash to see the list: `/modelo`, `/agente` and `/proyecto`. See "Commands and shortcuts".
