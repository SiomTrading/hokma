# Settings

Open Settings with the gear in the left menu or with Ctrl and a comma.

## Sections

- **General:** your name, your fund, language, theme and working folder.
- **Agents:** the team, their models and their switches.
- **Models and consumption:** how much of your weekly allowance you have used.
- **Extensions:** plugins, MCP servers and marketplaces.
- **System status:** the checks behind `hokma doctor`, on screen.
- **News:** what changed in each version, and the button to update.

## Weekly allowance

You set a weekly limit in tokens. Hokma adds the tokens of your chats and your scheduled tasks over the current
week and shows the percentage. The number is approximate: for the exact figure of your Claude plan use `/usage`.

## System status

It runs the same checks as `hokma doctor` plus the database. Each check shows its state and how to fix it.
Press "Check again" after you fix something.

## Update Hokma

In **News** you see the installed version and the notes of each release. If a newer version exists, "Update Hokma"
installs it. Close and open Hokma again to use it. Your work is never touched.
