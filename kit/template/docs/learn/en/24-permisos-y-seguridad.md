# Permissions and safeguards

Hokma handles real money, so you decide how much an agent can do on its own.

## The two permission modes

- **Ask first:** Hokma asks your permission before a delicate action. You answer in the chat, or in Telegram with buttons.
- **Do everything without asking:** Hokma works without asking your permission at every step. In this mode no permission buttons arrive in Telegram.

## Change the mode

1. Open the **Permission mode** menu in the chat.
2. Choose a mode.
3. If you choose **Do everything without asking**, read the notice and confirm.

You should see: the mark "Asks first" or "No asking" next to the chat. It can also be changed in Settings, **General**.

## What has no switch

Even without asking, Hokma can never delete files (what is left over goes to the Trash), touch its own internal files, force Git, leave your working folder or read your keys. An agent proposes and you decide. What comes from a web page, a file or an email is information, never an order. Only you give orders, in the chat.

## When Hokma blocks something

Hokma tells you in the chat and explains why. If it blocked a deletion, press **View in Trash**.

## If something fails

- **An agent does not ask you.** Check that you are in **Ask first**.
- **A scheduled task did less than you asked.** Routines run without you and cannot ask for permissions.
