# Chart

The chart screen shows candles for a symbol and lets you ask your fund about what you see.

## Read the chart

The header shows the symbol, the interval, the last price and the change. Up candles use the up color and down
candles the down color.

## Change the interval

Press one of the favorites (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1D) or press + and write any interval: 30s, 7m, 3h, 2D, 1W. Press Enter.
Press the star to pin the interval as a favorite.

## Opening high line

On intraday timeframes a dashed orange line marks the highest price of the first 30 minutes of the session of the
day. It is hidden on 1D.

## Open another symbol

In the plus menu, under Assets, search a symbol. Each symbol opens in its own tab. The symbols you can find depend
on the market data source connected to your Hokma.

## Change the symbol

Press the symbol in the header and search. The tab keeps its interval.

## What the chart remembers

Each chart tab remembers its symbol, its interval, its zoom, how far you scrolled and the price scale. Reload the page
or close the browser and the tab comes back as you left it.

- Scroll to the right edge and the chart follows: new candles enter on the right.
- Scroll back and new candles do not move your view.
- A new interval or a new symbol starts with the default view.
- Double-click the price scale to make it automatic again.

A new chart tab opens with the last chart you looked at: its symbol, interval and view. If that symbol is already open
in another tab, the new tab opens empty with the search.

This is saved in this browser only.

## Ask your fund about this chart

Press "Ask the fund about this chart". Hokma exports the chart as an image, attaches it to a new conversation with
the symbol and the timeframe, and opens the chat.
