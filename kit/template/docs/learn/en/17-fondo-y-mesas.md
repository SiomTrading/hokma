# The Fund and the money desks

The **Fund** is your home screen. It puts together all your desks, their capital and the **Today** card.

## Open the Fund

1. In the side menu press **Money desks**.
2. Open the **Fund** tab.

You should see: at the top the **Capital**, the sum of the capital of the accounts assigned to your desks. Below, the **Today** card and one card per desk with its account and its broker. The "Fund lessons" tab arrives in a later version.

## The Today card

It is shared by all your desks. Do not confuse it with each desk's result of today. It has four blocks:

- **Watchlist:** the assets of your desks, with no repeats, with price and change.
- **Economic calendar:** the time, the impact and the event.
- **Market news.**
- **Premarket briefing:** press **Generate now** to see it now.

To change it, press **Adjust with Hokma** and tell it in the chat what you want: extra assets, the minimum impact of the calendar, the news topics or the briefing time. Hokma applies the change when you give permission. If a data connection is missing, press **Go to Connections**.

## Create a desk

1. In the Fund press **+ Create desk**.
2. Choose **Create it with Hokma** or **From a template**.

With Hokma: tell it what you want to trade and with how much capital. It proposes the name, template, assets, mandate and budget, and creates the desk when you give permission.

With a template, follow the steps:

1. **Focus:** name, what it watches, capital, monthly goal and mandate.
2. **Team of agents:** **Minimal**, **Standard** or **With debate**.
3. **Rules:** the weekly token cap.
4. **Assets and data:** the symbols the desk follows (up to 20).
5. Press **Create desk**.

You should see: "Desk created." and the desk in the Fund. The account is assigned separately, in the **Broker** tab (see the "Broker and capital" topic).

## The desk's agents

1. Open the desk and press the **Agents** tab.
2. Choose an agent to see what it can and cannot do.
3. Change **Model on this desk**. It changes only on that desk.
4. Press **Bring an existing agent** to add one you already have, or **+ Create agent** for a new one.
5. To take one out, press **Remove from desk**. The agent is not deleted. The desk chief cannot be removed.

## Assets and the desk watchlist

1. Open the desk and press the **Terminal** tab.
2. Press **+ Add asset** and type the symbol, for example `AAPL` or `NQ=F`. Press Enter.
3. Press an asset in the list to see its chart.
4. Drag an asset, or use Alt and the arrows, to change the order.
5. To remove it, press **Remove** next to the symbol.

You can also edit the assets in the **Settings** tab. A desk follows up to 20. They are the only ones it can propose. The assets of all your desks appear in the watchlist of the Today card.

## Delete a desk

1. Open the desk and go to **Settings**.
2. In the **Danger zone** press **Delete desk**.
3. Type the desk's name to confirm.
4. Press **Delete desk**.

You should see: "Desk {name} deleted. Its folder is in the Trash." The folder can be restored from the Trash. The desk and its list of indexed documents do not come back. Your agents and conversations are not deleted.

## If something fails

- **The desk does not answer.** Check the token budget: at 100% the desk's chat and tasks do not start. Raise the cap in **Settings** or wait for next week.
- **An agent cannot be brought.** It is off in Settings. Press **Turn on and bring**.
- **"The Today card could not be loaded".** Try again with **Refresh**.
- **An asset says "No price".** Check your sources in Connections.
