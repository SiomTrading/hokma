# Voice: voice notes and transcription

Hokma turns your voice into text on your own computer. The audio never leaves your computer. It helps with long requests, dictating your journal and correcting without typing.

## Prepare voice (once)

1. In the chat, press **Record a voice note**.
2. If Hokma offers to download the voice model, press **Download**. It weighs a few hundred MB.
3. Wait. You can keep using Hokma.

You should see: "Model ready. Press the microphone to record."

## Dictate a message

1. Press **Record a voice note** and speak.
2. Press **Stop and transcribe**.
3. Review the text and send it.

To discard a recording, press **Cancel recording**. A transcript is text: you can summarize it, sort it and ask the agent to save it in an `.md` file.

## If something fails

- **"The browser has no permission to use the microphone".** Allow the microphone and try again.
- **"No microphone was found".** Plug one in and retry.
- **"No speech was heard".** Speak closer to the microphone and record again.
- **The model could not be downloaded.** Check your connection and press **Try again**.
- **"Voice is not available on this computer".** Send your `engine.log`: see "Troubleshooting".
