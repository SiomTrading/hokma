<h1>NORTHWIND INSTRUMENTS INC.</h1><p>FORM 10-K</p><p>For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023</p>
<p>PART I</p>
<p><b>Item 1. Business</b></p>
<p>Northwind designs and sells precision sensors for industrial automation. Most revenue comes from recurring calibration contracts.</p>
<p><b>Item 1A. Risk Factors</b></p>
<p>Our five largest customers account for 48 percent of revenue. The loss of one of them would reduce results.</p>
<p>Supply of specialty glass is concentrated in two vendors.</p>
<p><b>Item 1B. Unresolved Staff Comments</b></p>
<p>None.</p>
<p>PART II</p>
<p><b>Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations</b></p>
<p>Revenue grew 12 percent to 412.0 million dollars. Gross margin was 41.5 percent.</p>
