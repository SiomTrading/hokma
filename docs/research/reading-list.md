# Reading list

A reading list for the five lines of Hokma: DT (intraday futures), SWC (swing on CFDs), SWO (swing with listed options and premium selling), LP (long term portfolios of stocks, ETFs and crypto in hold) and RF (fixed income).

Rules of this page:
- Every book is listed once, under the line where it helps first. A book that also helps another line says so in brackets.
- No link in this file carries an affiliate or tracking parameter. A test fails if one appears.
- The links of the videos, blogs and courses are checked in CI by the workflow links.yml. The tests never touch the network.
- The origin of each list is registered in .notices/THIRD_PARTY.txt (reference READ-001).

## Books by line

### DT: intraday futures
Microstructure, execution and high frequency work. Read these before trusting any fill rule on minute data.

- How to Day Trade for a Living. Andrew Aziz.
- Day Trading QuickStart Guide. Troy Noonan.
- Algorithmic Trading and DMA. Barry Johnson.
- Trading and Exchanges. Larry Harris.
- Machine Trading. Ernest P. Chan. [also SWC]
- Inside the Black Box. Rishi K. Narang.
- Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading. Alvaro Cartea, Sebastian Jaimungal, Jose Penalva.
- The Problem of HFT. Haim Bodek.
- An Introduction to High-Frequency Finance. Ramazan Gencay, Michel Dacorogna, Ulrich A. Muller, Olivier Pictet, Richard Olsen.
- Market Microstructure in Practice. Charles-Albert Lehalle, Sophie Laruelle.
- The Financial Mathematics of Market Liquidity. Olivier Gueant.
- High-Frequency Trading. Maureen O'Hara, David Easley, Marcos Lopez de Prado.
- Algorithmic Trading Methods. Robert Kissell.
- Professional Automated Trading. Eugene A. Durenard. [also SWC]

### SWC: swing on CFDs
System design, trend following and the discipline to test a rule before trading it.

- Introduction To Algo Trading. Kevin J Davey. [also DT]
- Building Winning Algorithmic Trading Systems. Kevin J Davey. [also DT]
- Naked Forex. Alex Nekritin.
- The Art and Science of Technical Analysis. Adam Grimes.
- The New Trading for a Living. Alexander Elder.
- Systematic Trading. Robert Carver. [also LP]
- Algorithmic Trading. Ernest P. Chan. [also DT]
- Leveraged Trading. Robert Carver. [also LP]
- Trading Systems. Emilio Tomasini, Urban Jaekle.
- Trading Systems 2nd edition. Emilio Tomasini, Urban Jaekle.
- Quantitative Technical Analysis. Dr Howard B Bandy.
- Trading Evolved. Andreas F. Clenow. [also LP]

### SWO: swing with options
Volatility and pricing. It is the only book in this list written for option traders; read it together with the LP books for position sizing.

- Option Volatility and Pricing. Sheldon Natenberg.

### LP: long term portfolios
Portfolio construction, factor investing and investor behaviour.

- A Beginner's Guide to the Stock Market. Matthew R. Kratter.
- The Little Book of Common Sense Investing. John C. Bogle.
- Investing QuickStart Guide. Ted D. Snow.
- The Intelligent Investor. Benjamin Graham, Jason Zweig.
- How I Invest My Money. Joshua Brown, Brian Portnoy.
- The Four Pillars of Investing. William J. Bernstein. [also RF]
- Quantitative Momentum. Wesley R. Gray, Jack R. Vogel.
- Quantitative Equity Portfolio Management. Ludwig B Chincarini, Daehwan Kim.
- Active Portfolio Management. Richard Grinold, Ronald Kahn. [also RF]
- Advances in Active Portfolio Management. Richard Grinold, Ronald Kahn. [also RF]
- Algorithmic Trading and Quantitative Strategies. Raja Velu, Maxence Hardy, Daniel Nehren. [also all lines]
- Quantitative Trading. Xin Guo, Tze Leung Lai, Howard Shek, Samuel Po-Shing Wong. [also all lines]

### RF: fixed income
No book of the source list is written for fixed income, so none is listed only here. The books marked [also RF] cover allocation and risk for bond portfolios. For rules, read the bonds section of the edge table (backend/hokma/factory/data/literature_edges.csv) and the papers it points to.
